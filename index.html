🔋 Understanding Optimal Battery Ranges

Lithium-ion batteries experience standard electrochemical stress at high states of charge (above 80%) and low states of charge (below 20%). By keeping your battery within the 20% to 80% sweet spot, you can extend your device's overall battery life span by up to 2-3x!

📱 Progressive Web App (PWA) Background Playback

Browsers restrict background processing strictly. To ensure continuous monitoring, follow these steps:

🤖 For Android Users (Chrome / Edge)

  • Press the Install App button in the header or selection drawer to install it to your home screen.
  • Tap Engage Guardian Shield to active audio and notification authorization.
  • Disable "Battery Optimization" or "Background App Restrictions" in your phone's Settings specifically for Battery Guardian to prevent Android from sleeping the app.
  • Keep the screen awake using the Wake Lock switch inside the dashboard!

🍎 For iOS Users (Safari)

  • Tap the Share button in Safari (bottom navigation bar).
  • Select Add to Home Screen.
  • Open the installed "Battery Guard" app from your home screen.
  • Tap Engage Guardian Shield once to unlock Apple Web Audio playback permissions.
  • Note: iOS doesn't support the Battery API. Use the **Simulator mode** within the app to run simulated warnings, or pin the app open with Wake Lock active during overnight charging!
⚠️ Crucial Requirement: Audio alarms cannot trigger autonomously on first load. You MUST click "Engage Guardian Shield" to grant audio playback authorization.
